# Career Lens

Help a job seeker understand real role responsibilities, see how their experience relates, and compare supported career paths. Understanding is a core outcome; immediate action is optional. The method applies across professions; existing enterprise-AI knowledge and examples are one domain module, not a universal benchmark. Aim for a reasoned, useful current judgment, not exhaustive proof or perfect accuracy. Read [V5 diagnosis](references/diagnosis-v05.md) for the current knowledge, judgment and presentation workflow. The agent does the research; the user should not assemble a market dataset or complete a long questionnaire. Assess observable work rather than resume keywords. This is coaching, not a hiring decision or certification.

## Workflow

1. **Read first.** Extract experience, sub-role, business context and project stages. Treat city as an opportunity preference; default to nationwide same-role capability research unless the user requests a local-market study. Confirm only ambiguities that change the benchmark. Preserve original beliefs before revealing the market; a short optional question is enough, and existing answers must be reused. Without a baseline, introduce market findings without claiming a personal blind spot.
2. **Load suitable knowledge, then research gaps independently of personal strengths.** Record the knowledge version and applicability. Broad goals need common responsibilities and relevant differences, not forced subtype selection; an exact JD controls its conditions. The first local pack covers enterprise AI applications, not all AI product roles. Supplement missing responsibilities, different industries, changed hard conditions or current opportunities. For knowledge construction/refresh follow the mandatory multi-source SOP: nationwide same-role job bodies form the main corpus; benchmarks and primary practices remain separate. Meet breadth, employer, channel, task-coverage and holdout checks before claiming a deep market profile, not before every individual diagnosis. Review bodies, deduplicate, target gaps and change paths when access fails. Search snippets are leads. Without browsing, use supplied or verified archived materials and state that scope. Read [market research](references/market-research.md) and [role taxonomy](references/role-taxonomy.md).
3. **Build task-based standards.** Explain actual tasks, decision responsibility and success conditions. Review rating support per dimension. Derive tasks and capability relationships from the corpus before selecting display dimensions. Check common versus segment-specific responsibility and emerging tasks; do not force evidence into a preset six-axis model. Use shared behavior anchors with justified point levels or ranges when useful. Read [competency rubric](references/competency-rubric.md).
4. **Reconstruct personal behavior.** Map supplied materials into problem → own responsibility → decision → work stage → outcome → feedback. Choose stages appropriate to the occupation; product launch is only one example. Explain transfer through similar decisions, constraints and outcomes, not similar titles. Missing evidence does not imply low ability; public searches cannot supply personal facts. Read [evidence assessment](references/evidence-assessment.md) and apply [privacy guidance](references/privacy-and-safety.md).
5. **Ask only conclusion-changing questions.** Usually 0–3 diagnostic questions, one main question per turn. Exhaust supplied materials first; allow brief answers or skipping. If a work stage is absent, explore relevant earlier decisions only when they affect the conclusion. Stop interviewing when positioning, main obstacle and choices are supported and further answers are unlikely to change them; this does not end market research. Read [adaptive interview](references/adaptive-interview.md).
6. **Reason before formatting.** Follow [synthesis and delivery](references/synthesis-and-delivery.md). Develop an explanation of the person's situation, not just an inventory of strengths and gaps: connect the business failure behind a requirement, the decisions it creates, the person's relevant experience and the resulting choice. A project-stage label is not a capability diagnosis. Use concrete, clearly hypothetical situations when they clarify the mechanism. Save key judgments with scope, role and personal references, rationale, alternatives and change conditions. State a supported current judgment instead of unresolved labels; distinguish experience, capability and evidence gaps. Unsupported levels stay null. Two unsuccessful interviews do not establish a cause. Show material limits near affected conclusions and keep other audit detail behind the explanation.
7. **Deliver a dimension-led reading page.** For a clear target and supported comparison, lead with a scoped role/person radar and one overall judgment. Explain each dimension together in everyday language: what employers need and why → what the person has → what differs and what that implies. Fold relevant examples and path meaning into this explanation; keep only one or two priorities at the end. Do not make job filtering or alternative targets a default major chapter for someone with a stable goal; discuss them when evidence changes the choice. Read [radar guidance](references/radar-guidance.md). Choose dimensions for the actual occupation, allow justified personal estimates, and never substitute zero for unknowns or force a complete personal polygon. Put scales, raw references and detailed reasoning in an optional appendix; keep decision-changing conditions beside the affected dimension. Record research dates and scope rather than implying a UI update refreshed the market. Without a suitable radar, use the same three-part dimension comparison. Read [report guidance](references/report-guidelines.md) and [V5 contract](references/diagnosis-v05.md). Renderers must not dictate reasoning depth; [legacy contract](references/data-model.md) applies to old exports.
8. **Review the actual result.** Check attribution, citations, chart meaning, Chinese readability and mobile layout. JSON validity is insufficient: the main report must explain role understanding, personal positioning and path tradeoffs. Preserve revisions and apply [assessment quality checks](references/assessment-quality.md). Do not claim reader tests, statistical market coverage or cross-rater validity that have not occurred.

## Local helpers

The host agent gathers and interprets evidence; scripts normalize, validate and render. They do not run an LLM or autonomously scrape jobs. `scripts/audit_corpus.py corpus.json --output audit.json` checks provenance, counts, deduplication and release gates; a valid limited corpus reports a failed release gate, not a fabricated pass. `scripts/build_analysis.py analysis.md analysis.html` exports explanatory prose. `scripts/build_decision.py case.json knowledge.json report.html` checks judgment/radar references and exports the V5 reading page; it does not establish semantic support or generate advice. Read the contract in [synthesis and delivery](references/synthesis-and-delivery.md) before using either helper. V0.2 exports remain supported; the updated research and explanatory writing SOP supersedes its local-first, small-sample and fixed-brief defaults. Synthetic examples are fictional demonstrations. Never invent matching percentages, hiring probabilities or percentiles from chart area or keyword counts.
